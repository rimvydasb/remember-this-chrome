design proposal · v2

Graphite

A solid, dynamic workspace for the library. Dark by default, monospace for data, one indigo accent. The whole width goes to content: no inspector column and no status bar. AI state lives in one header control that opens a detailed panel.

01Collapsible filtersA Filters button opens a panel above the list; each group folds on its own. When closed, active filters show as removable chips in the same row.
01bCollapsible sidebarFolds to an icon rail; catalogs keep letter badges.
01cAligned sub-headerOne 44 px band across all columns with a single straight bottom line.
01dPlain transcriptPlain paragraphs, no timestamps, exactly as stored and sent to the AI.
02Local AI panelA header pill shows AI state and progress; click it for server, model, queue and controls.
03Tag chips unchangedSame colors and icons as the app today: mood colors, category icons, red channel, navy topics.
04No inspectorFacts, sentiment, chapters and links are part of the main column.
05Settings & prompt editorPrompt editor with line numbers, field palette, validation, live preview and diff against the default.
06Bookmark + star iconFlat, no gradients: five variants to choose from.
07Sections, not tabsSummary, sentiment, questions, related, details and transcript are stacked sections that fold.
08Theme & settings placementTop-right of the header, marked with pins below.
09No footer barContent runs to the bottom of the window.

App icon

Flat colors only, no gradients. The bookmark and the star have rounded corners; a soft shadow lifts the bookmark off the tile. Each variant is shown on a dark and a light toolbar at 128, 32 and 16 px. Click a variant to use it everywhere on this page.

A · Indigo tileWhite bookmark, star cut out. Strongest at 16 px. Recommended.
B · Graphite tileToday’s design, flat: dark tile, white bookmark, indigo star.
C · NightDark tile, indigo bookmark, star cut out.
D · Bookmark onlyNo tile: the bookmark is the icon. Lightest look.
E · PaperWhite tile, graphite bookmark, star cut out.

Tag chips

Unchanged from the app: the icon shows the kind of sentiment, the color shows the mood (green → lime → gray / yellow → orange → red). Channels are red with a person icon; topics are navy. A filled chip is an active filter.

Aligned sub-header. The first row under the header is one band, 44 px high, in all three columns: Library + collapse, Filters + active chips + count + sort, and breadcrumb + item actions. Their bottom lines form one straight line; nothing wraps onto a second row.

Filters: closed and open

The row height never changes. Closed, the list gets all the height and the active filters stay in the same row as removable chips (they fade out at the edge when there are many; the badge still counts them). Open, each group can fold; “any of” and “all of” are written next to the group name. The state is remembered.
